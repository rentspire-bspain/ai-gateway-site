Refunds, reversals and disputes

BoundView · Updated November 26, 2026

Trials

Paid plans start with a 30-day free trial. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you are never charged — cancel in Settings → Billing or from the link in your receipt email. If a trial converts and you intended to cancel, contact support within 7 days for a full refund of that first charge.

Monthly plans

Cancel any time in Settings → Billing; access continues to the end of the paid period. For billing errors or accidental duplicate charges, contact support and we will reverse the charge. This page does not create rights beyond our Terms.

Neurons (earned credits)

Neurons are earn-only rewards for AI usage. They are never purchased, never transferable, and have no cash value — so there is nothing to refund or cash out. If a partner offer you completed did not credit, use the dispute route in the earn tab (offers not credited within 24 hours) rather than a payment dispute.

Disputes and chargebacks

Please contact support@boundview.com before initiating a payment dispute — nearly everything is fixable directly and faster. Our terms cap certain remedies at amounts you paid us in the prior 12 months or $100, whichever is greater, where law allows.
