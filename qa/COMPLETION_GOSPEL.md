# COMPLETION GOSPEL — the standing law of completion monitoring and tracking

**Owner directive, codified 2026-11-17 by Brett Spain. These rules are binding on every agent,
guardian, automation, build lane, and subagent producing completion documents, registers, checklists,
gate tables, audit records, or status reports — on ai.spaincos.com, BoundView.com, the PHA Solutions
suite (RentSpire), and every sibling in the Spain Companies platform, including Perplexity sessions
and any external tool that tracks completion. They are version-controlled in this repo and may only
be changed by owner directive.**

## The creed

**The icons are the gospel on status.** A completion state is communicated with the actual icons —
🟢 🟡 🔴 — never the words GREEN, YELLOW, RED, and never the abbreviations G/R/Y. If a status
document spells out a color, it is wrong and is regenerated.

## The law (binding rules)

1. **Icons, always.** Every completion document, register, checklist, gate table, audit record,
   dashboard, or status report uses 🟢 for shipped and verified, 🟡 for partial / evidence-owed /
   in-progress, and 🔴 for open, blocking, or unproven. In contexts that cannot render emoji
   (plain-text audit exports, CSV), the explicit words `DONE`, `PARTIAL`, `OPEN` are the only
   permitted substitutes — never color names.

2. **Strikethrough is the completion marker.** An item is struck through only when it is shipped
   **and** verified. A struck item may keep an unstruck residual, which remains open and is never
   hidden by the strike.

3. **No optimistic inference.** An absence is an explicit zero or a stated "not configured," never a
   blank that reads as done. A partial state is 🟡 with the missing evidence named — not 🟢 with a
   caveat buried in prose.

4. **One truthful master.** When multiple trackers exist, one is the master and the others link to
   it; status may be copied but never paraphrased into something rosier than the master's row.

5. **Every status carries its evidence.** A 🟢 names its verification (live check, PR, run, receipt).
   A 🟡 names the remaining work. A 🔴 names the blocker and the owner of the next action.

6. **Scope.** This law applies wherever completion monitoring or tracking is produced — including
   Perplexity sessions and Computer deliverables, the SHMT master registers and doctrine tables,
   rr-pro `docs/RULES.md` (Rule 41), the PHA Solutions completion register, BoundView parity and
   QA reports, and any future status surface. Where a repo has its own rules file, the gospel is
   mirrored there; the mirrors may add detail but never soften this law.
