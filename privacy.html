Privacy Policy

ai.spaincos.com · Last updated November 10, 2026

This is a private site. Access is limited to explicitly authorized accounts. There is no public sign-up, no advertising, no analytics trackers, and no sale of data. This policy explains what is stored and why.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and authentication data, held by Supabase Auth.
  • Your content: threads, messages, attachments, saved files, projects, and settings you create while using the site. These are stored in the site's Supabase database and are visible only to your account (enforced by row-level security).
  • Operational logs: security and rate-limiting records (e.g., request counts, error telemetry) used to keep the service healthy and safe.
  • Local browser storage: your sign-in session and UI preferences stay on your device.

How your queries are processed

To answer questions, your queries (and any attached files) are processed by AI model providers (such as OpenRouter-hosted models) and, when relevant, by web search. These providers process content to generate answers under their own policies; we do not send them anything beyond what is needed to answer your query.

Third parties

Infrastructure providers involved in running the site: Supabase (database, auth, functions), Cloudflare/edge hosting for network delivery, and the AI providers named above. No advertising networks, no data brokers, no trackers.

Your control

  • Delete any thread, file, or project directly in the app at any time.
  • Request full account deletion by emailing bspain@spaincos.com — your account and all content will be removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: bspain@spaincos.com
