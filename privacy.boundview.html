BoundView Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · BoundView (boundview.com)

BoundView is an AI research workspace that rewards participation with Neurons, our earn-only reward currency. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It replaces the earlier workspace policy on this page, which described a private, invite-only service and is no longer accurate for BoundView.

The short version: we collect what we need to run your account, your research history, and your Neurons balance. When you complete partner offers or use affiliate links, those partners process the activity needed to credit your rewards. We do not sell your personal information. Neurons are earn-only and cannot be cashed out by us.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, password (hashed), school or signup verification status, and account settings.
  • Usage: your threads, searches, uploaded documents, and Neurons ledger entries (earn, spend, expiry).
  • Offer and affiliate activity: when you complete a partner offer (surveys, tasks, trials) or use an affiliate link, the partner network tells us which transaction completed so we can credit your Neurons. The postback we receive contains your BoundView user ID, a transaction ID, and the reward amount — not your email or password.
  • Technical: request logs (IP, user agent, timestamps) for security, abuse prevention, and rate limits.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the product: run your research, keep your history, maintain your Neurons balance.
  • To detect fraud and enforce earning caps (for example, daily and monthly offer limits).
  • To send service email (verification, security notices, essential account communications).

3. Offer-wall and affiliate partners (FTC disclosure)

BoundView earns revenue from affiliate commissions and offer-wall networks (for example, Adscend Media and ayeT-Studios). When you complete an offer:

  • The partner sees the activity on its own platform under the anonymous ID you brought to it.
  • The partner sends BoundView a signed server-to-server confirmation containing only your user ID and reward amount.
  • BoundView credits your Neurons balance from that confirmation. We verify each confirmation cryptographically and cap rewards per user.

Partners' own privacy policies govern their platforms. Completing offers is always optional; Neurons are never required to use BoundView's core research features.

4. Neurons

Neurons are an earn-only reward currency for use inside BoundView (for example, premium model access). They have no cash value, cannot be redeemed for cash by us, and may expire per the terms shown with each earn action.

5. Cookies and storage

We use browser storage for your session and preferences, and analytics that do not build advertising profiles of you. We do not run third-party advertising cookies on BoundView.

6. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run BoundView (hosting, email delivery, model providers processing your research requests) and offer/affiliate partners as described in section 3. We do not sell personal information.

7. Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Thread content follows the retention settings in your workspace. Request logs are kept for a limited security window.

8. Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your account data from Settings, or by contacting us below.
  • California residents: you may request disclosure of categories of information collected and opt out of any sale — we do not sell, so no opt-out is needed.
  • You may stop offer participation at any time without affecting your account.

9. Children

BoundView is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable), and offer surfaces require minimum-age eligibility per each partner's terms.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed credentials, signed partner postbacks, per-user caps, and least-privilege service access. No system is perfectly secure; report anything suspicious.

11. Contact

BoundView is operated by CanonIntel LLC, the data controller for this service.

Privacy questions: support@boundview.com · © 2026 CanonIntel LLC

12. Your privacy rights (state laws)

If you are a resident of California (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, or another state with a comparable privacy law, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" as those terms are defined by law. We do not sell personal information. The only third-party advertising surface is the partner offer wall, which is off by default: it loads only after you explicitly accept it in the Earn tab, and rejecting it (or sending a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser or device) keeps it off. To exercise any right, email support@boundview.com from your account address; we will verify and respond within the statutory period. We will never discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
