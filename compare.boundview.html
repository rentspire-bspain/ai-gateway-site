When to pick something else

BoundView does not aim to out-scale frontier labs. If you need the widest ecosystem of third-party integrations or frontier-only capabilities, a Big-Three subscription may fit better. If you want sourced answers on open-weight routing with visible pricing and earn-only rewards, BoundView is built for you.
