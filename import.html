Import your AI history

Bring your conversations from Perplexity, Claude, or ChatGPT. Imported threads appear in your sidebar and give the assistant full context in every new conversation.

Perplexity

  1. Go to Settings → Data (perplexity.ai/settings/data).
  2. Click Export data and confirm.
  3. You'll receive an email with a download link — download the ZIP and open it.
  4. Inside, find conversations.json — that's the file to upload below.

Claude

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy (claude.ai/settings/privacy).
  2. Under "Export data", request your data export.
  3. Download the ZIP when the email arrives and open it.
  4. Upload conversations.json below.

ChatGPT / OpenAI

  1. Go to Settings → Data controls → Export data (chatgpt.com/#settings).
  2. Click Export — you'll get an email with a download link.
  3. Download and unzip it.
  4. Upload conversations.json below.
